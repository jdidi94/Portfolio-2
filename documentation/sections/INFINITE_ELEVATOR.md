# Neon Portfolio

## Infinite Elevator Experience

Version 1.0

---

# Overview

The Infinite Elevator is the interactive timeline experience of the Neon Portfolio.

Instead of scrolling through years, the visitor ascends through an endless vertical journey where each platform represents a major milestone in my career.

The elevator symbolizes continuous growth, lifelong learning, and the belief that there is always another level to reach.

There is no visible beginning or end.

The journey always continues.

---

# Experience Vision

The visitor enters a massive dark void.

Only a single illuminated platform exists beneath them.

Far above, dozens of glowing platforms slowly disappear into darkness.

Far below, previous milestones fade into the distance.

Soft ambient music plays.

Particles drift upward.

The elevator silently begins to rise.

The visitor is no longer browsing a portfolio.

They are climbing through my professional journey.

---

# Design Philosophy

Growth is never linear.

Every platform represents effort, learning, failure, and achievement.

The empty space between platforms symbolizes the time, persistence, and work required to reach the next stage.

The visitor experiences progress rather than simply reading dates.

# Camera Behaviour

The camera is attached to the elevator.

Movement characteristics

* Smooth acceleration
* Soft deceleration
* Gentle breathing
* Slight floating
* Cinematic easing

Never teleport.

Every movement should feel heavy and realistic.

---

# Elevator Motion

The elevator continuously moves upward.

Stopping occurs only when:

* Hovering a milestone
* Selecting a milestone
* Opening project details

Movement should resume naturally after interaction.

# Hover Interaction

Approaching a platform causes:

* Platform glow increases
* Edge lights brighten
* Year becomes visible
* Ambient particles intensify
* Elevator slows naturally

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The Infinite Elevator is complete when:

* The elevator moves smoothly through the timeline.
* Each platform represents one meaningful milestone.
* Camera transitions feel cinematic.
* Hover interactions slow the elevator naturally.
* Clicking opens detailed milestone content.
* Empty space enhances immersion rather than feeling unfinished.
* The final platform invites future collaboration.
* The experience integrates seamlessly with the rest of the Neon Portfolio.

---

# Definition of Success

The visitor should not feel like they are reading a résumé.

They should feel like they are ascending through the story of a developer who continuously learns, builds, and reaches for the next level.

The elevator should leave one lasting impression:

> **The journey isn't over—the best milestone could be the one we build together.**
