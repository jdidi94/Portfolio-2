# Neon Portfolio

## Product Vision v1.0

---

# What This Is

Neon Portfolio is a premium immersive 3D product experience.

It is **not** a traditional website.

It is **not** a game.

It is an interactive digital showcase built to demonstrate software engineering skill, product thinking, interaction design, and technical craftsmanship.

The portfolio itself is the proof.

---

# Problem

Recruiters and hiring managers review many portfolios that look the same:

* Static layouts
* Generic templates
* Scroll-and-scan content dumps
* Little evidence of systems thinking or craft

A strong engineer needs a medium that communicates taste, architecture, and interaction quality — not only a list of skills.

---

# Solution

Visitors do not scroll. Visitors travel.

Navigation happens through cinematic camera movement inside a minimal futuristic environment: infinite black space, floating objects, intentional light, and calm motion.

Every technical choice reinforces the image of an engineer who can ship premium web experiences.

---

# Audience

Primary

* Technical recruiters
* Engineering hiring managers
* Senior engineers evaluating craft

Secondary

* Design-minded product partners
* Anyone assessing frontend depth in a short session

---

# Success Definition

The portfolio succeeds when:

* A recruiter understands the developer’s profile within **five minutes**
* The experience runs smoothly on common desktop hardware
* Interactions feel intentional, never accidental
* Projects are easy to explore without UI clutter
* Technical ability is visible without sacrificing usability
* Visitors remember the experience after leaving

North-star outcome:

**Someone schedules an interview.**

---

# Experience Principles

The experience must feel:

* Premium
* Smooth
* Calm
* Elegant
* Interactive
* Memorable

Avoid:

* Visual clutter
* Excessive effects
* Flashing lights
* Fast or mechanical camera movement
* Unnecessary UI

Darkness is part of the design. Negative space is intentional. Every animation must have purpose.

---

# Interaction Model

* No traditional navigation bar, sidebar, or large menus
* Camera travel replaces scrolling
* UI appears only when necessary and disappears when possible
* Hover, focus, and selection are precise and quiet
* Audio is minimal and event-driven — never overwhelming

---

# Content Journey

Sections (same visual language throughout):

1. Hero
2. About
3. Projects
4. Skills
5. Experience
6. Timeline
7. Contact

Project cards expand in place without breaking orientation. Content arrives after the experience engine is solid — systems before substance.

---

# Visual Direction

Environment

* Infinite black space
* No terrain, floor, walls, or skybox
* Floating objects only
* Soft fog, subtle particles, neon accents

Palette

* Black / dark gray / white
* Electric blue / purple / cyan

Materials

* Glass, metallic, reflective, emissive
* Avoid plastic, cartoon, flat fills

Motion

* Everything moves subtly
* Cards float and react to hover
* Camera breathes gently
* Nothing teleports

---

# Product Constraints

* Desktop-first (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari)
* Responsive support after desktop is complete
* 60 FPS minimum; 120 FPS preferred on capable hardware
* Accessibility: reduced motion, keyboard navigation, readable contrast, semantic HTML overlays
* Deploy on Vercel from GitHub with automatic deployments

---

# What We Will Not Build

* A scroll-driven marketing site dressed as 3D
* A game with combat, scores, or free-roam chaos
* Feature sprawl that does not help interview conversion
* Magic numbers, duplicated systems, or animation state in global stores

---

# Guiding Principle

Every line of code, every animation, every sound, and every interaction must contribute to one objective:

**Create a portfolio that convinces someone to schedule an interview.**

If a feature does not improve that goal, it does not belong.
